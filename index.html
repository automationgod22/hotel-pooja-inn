Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you plan your stay at Hotel Pooja Inn Pandharpur.

How far is Hotel Pooja Inn from Shri Vitthal Mandir? ▼

The hotel is approximately 1.8 km (around 5 to 7 minutes) from the main temple complex. Auto-rickshaws are readily available right outside the hotel at all times of day and night.

Is secure car parking available at the hotel? ▼

Yes! Unlike hotels located right in the narrow alleys near the temple where driving and parking are nearly impossible, Hotel Pooja Inn provides ample on-site private parking for private cars, SUVs, and passenger vans.

Is hot water available at 4:00 AM for Kakad Aarti? ▼

Yes, all our rooms have 24/7 hot water supply via geysers, specially ensured for pilgrims who wake up early for Kakad Aarti and holy snan.

Is there a lift (elevator) for elderly family members? ▼

Yes, our property is equipped with a modern elevator, making it very comfortable for senior citizens, grandparents, and warkari devotees to access upper floor rooms without climbing stairs.
