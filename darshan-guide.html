Mukh Darshan vs Charansparsh Queue

Understanding which queue to choose for you and your family:

QUICKEST (30 TO 60 MINS)

मुख दर्शन (Mukh Darshan)

In Mukh Darshan, you see Lord Vitthal and Goddess Rukmini from a distance of a few feet without touching the feet. This line moves very quickly and is ideal for senior citizens, young children, or those on a tight travel schedule.

DEEPEST DEVOTION (3 TO 6+ HOURS)

चरणस्पर्श (Charansparsh)

Every devotee gets to place their head directly on Lord Vitthal’s holy feet and receive Charan-teertha. The queue passes through the Darshan Mandap. During Ekadashi and weekends, this queue takes several hours, so plan ample resting time at Hotel Pooja Inn!

Taking a holy bath in the crescent-shaped Chandrabhaga (Bhima) river cleanses past karmas. Pundalik Temple is situated right in the riverbed.

3.2 km away (8 mins)

A serene ashram and temple situated on the tranquil bank of the Chandrabhaga River in Gopalpur. Famous for its pure vegetarian prasadam and goshala.

🪨

Vishnupad Temple

2.8 km away (7 mins)

Here, footprints of Lord Krishna and footprints of cows and calves are carved into the sacred rock in the riverbed.
